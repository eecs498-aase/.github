# Applied Agentic Software Engineering

We write it **AASE** and say it "A two S E."

A 4-credit University of Michigan course on one object, a coding agent, taken three ways across fifteen weeks. Use one to build one. Take the human out of its loop and measure what broke. Then grow it into an assistant worth keeping.

## The three phases

| Phase | Weeks | Share of grade | Focus | You ship |
|-------|-------|--------|-------|----------|
| Administrative | 1–15 | 10% | Lecture and lab attendance, plus incentive bonuses | |
| **Apply** | 1–3, build to end of 4 | 18% | Aider on a model you serve yourself. The Big Three: context, model, prompt. | A pair-programmer, seven fixed features, due Fri Sep 25 |
| **Analyze** | 4–7 | 22.5% | Raw LLM API calls, tool use, the agent loop, an approval layer, stop conditions | Agent v0 with an eval suite, due end of week 7 |
| **Create** | 8–15 | 49.5% | A wiki it can read, design skills it can run, hardening, deployment | An assistant worth keeping, demoed Thu Dec 10 |

The three phases divide the 90% that is not administrative in the ratio 20 / 25 / 55, which is where 18 / 22.5 / 49.5 comes from.

**No exams.** Projects, lab checkoffs, three evening hackathons, and a final showcase. All work is individual.

**It costs nothing.** No textbook, no API bill, no subscription. You serve a small model on your own machine, and CAEN covers you if your laptop cannot.
